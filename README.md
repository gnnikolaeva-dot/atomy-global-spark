# Global Atomy Ascent

Create a modern, high-converting, Western-style SaaS/Fintech landing page and multi-section

web application for a global business platform built on top of the Atomy ecosystem.

DESIGN & SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:

- Visual Style: Sleek dark theme (#0b0f19 background), deep navy cards (#161e2e), glowing

accents in Cyan (#38bdf8), Emerald Green (#10b981), and Indigo/Purple gradients. Clean

typography (Inter/SF Pro look), glassmorphism effects, subtle grid patterns.

- Target Audience: Global entrepreneurs, ambitious individuals tired of traditional 9-to-5

jobs, people seeking financial security and reliable residual income.

- Key Conversion Goal: Primary CTA across all sections leads to booking a "30-Minute

Strategy Video Call" with top mentor Galina Nikolaeva.

STRUCTURE & PAGES/SECTIONS:

1. Navigation Bar (Sticky): Logo ("ATOMY GLOBAL ENGINE"), Nav links (System, Global Scale,

Mentor, Math/Calculator, Strategy Call), Accent CTA Button ("Book 30-Min Strategy Call").

2. Hero Section: Premium headline, interactive badge ("Global Business System"), dynamic

earnings display, twin action buttons, live social proof counter.

3. Global Company Ecosystem (Atomy Stats): Founded in 2009, Top-10 Global Direct Selling

enterprise, operating in 170+ countries with absolute quality & absolute price philosophy.

4. Mentor Spotlight Section: Introducing Galina Nikolaeva (30+ years in MLM, former Oriflame

Gold Director, top leader) and her proven mentorship framework.

5. The Duplication System (The 60K PV Model): Explaining the simple 2-step monthly KPI

(60,000 PV personal shopping + recruiting 2 active partners) leading to 91,000 RUB

(~$1,000+) passive monthly income within 6 months.

6. Interactive Income & Duplication Calculator: Slider-based calculator visualising binary

steps, structure growth, and projected 6-month earnings.

7. Consultation Funnel & Process Steps: Step 1 (30-Min Video Call) -> Step 2 (Personalized

Roadmap) -> Step 3 (System Activation & 10K PV Start).

8. Mentorship Booking Modal & Contact Section: Interactive calendar picker / booking form

for the video call.
